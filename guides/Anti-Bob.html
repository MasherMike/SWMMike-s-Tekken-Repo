Bob on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: sidestep / sidewalk RIGHT (SSR / SWR). Wavu documents whiff-vs-step behavior on 11 of Bob's strings. Five of them whiff to your right, three to your left, and four whiff to either side. Stepping right beats strictly more of his committal enders, including two of his most common block-pressure strings (db4,4 and b4,3).

The cost of that default: his uf1 is specifically noted as having good right sidestep tracking, so a Bob who knows the matchup checks your SWR with it. It's only i16 / -9 and leads to uf1,2, so the punishment for getting caught is small — but it's the read he has.

Stance Counterplay

BAL — Balance (Spinner Ball)

Entered with f+3+4 (high crush frames 8~48), or with F off 1+2 (+1), df4,3 (+12g/+0), b2,1 (+12g on hit), b4,3 (-1/+25a), u+3 (+7/+12g/+16c) and u+1+2 (+0/+8). He cannot block in BAL. With no input the stance drops to full crouch.
Mid | i15~17 | -5 | His fastest option from stance
Extensions: ,2 (HIGH, +1 oB, Heat Engager — duck it) | H.BAL.1,4 (Heat-only, +4, attack throw on front hit)
Mid | i23~24 | -8 | Homing | Strong Aerial Tailspin | Balcony Break
Mid | i24~25 | +4 | Instant Tornado on CH | Low crush 21~25
LOW | i23~26 | -24 | High crush 1~55 | Side switch on CH
Mid | i29~33 | -13c | Hip | Floor Break | WS launcher punish
How to deal with it: He has exactly one low from BAL — BAL.4, at i23 and -24 on block. Everything else is mid or high, and the fastest is i15. So stand block is the default and it is barely a decision: the low is slow enough to react to and the punishment for blocking it is a full launch.

The one thing to respect is that he can't block in the stance. When he enters BAL at disadvantage or neutral — off 1+2 (+1), u+1+2 (+0), b4,3 (-1) — your i10 jab beats his i15 BAL.1 outright. Take your turn instead of waiting. When he enters at real advantage (u+3 at +7, df4,3 at +12g) block the mid and duck BAL.1,2 on the read.
