Zafina on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk RIGHT (SSR / SWR). This disagrees with the chart. The @Mezerdie sidestep chart calls SSL for Zafina. The per-move data (Tier 1) points right, and under this database's evidence rule Tier 1 wins. The raw tally is close: four strings whiff to your right, three to your left. What tips it is weight. Two of the four right-whiffs are her Scarecrow strings, and Scarecrow is her main mixup stance. Wavu's counterplay page says it in prose: sidestepping right is "very strong against scarecrow." Her most-used high, f+3 (+3 on block, goes into Scarecrow), also has "good left sidestep tracking", so it punishes an SSL habit. Worth a lab session to settle.

The exceptions go left. Her Mantis low string MNT.d+1,4, db+4,2 and f+2,3,4 are all documented whiffing to your left. If she's sitting in Mantis, SSL is the better step. Don't step her homing tools (list below). Most of them are highs, so duck them instead.

Stance Counterplay

SCR — Scarecrow

Entered raw with 3+4, or from 3 (+0), f+3 (+3), 1,2,3 (0), 1,3 (-2), f+2,3 (+0), df+3 (-5), db+3 (-8, low), df+1,2,1 / db+2,1 (-12), ws3, MNT.1,3, and the B-cancels off f,F+3 (+4) and f,f,F+3 (+3). The frames above are for the base moves. Her actual frames in stance depend on the entry.
Can she block? Not in the stance. Wavu: "Can block after 21 frames with db", meaning she has to cancel out to block. Holding back also cancels it.
Lows: a lot of them. SCR.b+1+2 (i16, -18), SCR.d+3 (i18, -6, safe), SCR.df+4 (i19~20, -14), SCR.d+4 (i24~30, -25), SCR.df+3 (i28~30, -23, homing).
Fastest move: SCR.1 at i10~11, a high. Her fastest mids are SCR.3 (i13) and SCR.2 (i14).

How to deal with it (Wavu): her Scarecrow mixup is only guaranteed when she enters it at +9. At +7 you still have counterplay, and "when she isn't at +9 powercrushes beat most of her options." Your other answers are SSR, which is strongest when she delays or holds the stance, and a crouch jab, which ducks SCR.1 and stuffs her slow lows. Watch for the cancel: holding back cancels the stance at worse frames, and good Zafinas use that to bait your power crush.

Heat

Heat Smash | High, special mid | i12~13 | +3 oB | 10 chip | Jails from 1st block | Attack throw on hit | Also from SCR
Plus on block, so there's no punish and you shouldn't press. But it starts high: duck it on read and it misses
Heat Burst | Mid | i16 | +1 oB | Also from SCR
What Heat changes (Azazel's Power): outside Heat, her weapon moves cost her own health. That includes f+1+2, df+4,1, b+1+2, SCR.df+1 and SCR.b+1+2, at 8–18 self-damage each. In Heat they're free, and each one she lands builds an ORB that unlocks Anathema follow-ups costing Heat time. Expect her to throw the weapon moves much more freely once she's in Heat.

General Counter Play

Deny the Scarecrow entries. Her best mixups come from Scarecrow, and her common ways in are highs: 3, f+3, 1,2,3, 1,3 and the ,3 of f+2,3. Ducking those on read stops the stance before it starts. When she does get in, check her frames. Below +9 her mix isn't guaranteed, and a power crush, a crouch jab or SSR beats most of it.
