Anti-Victor  |  SW Right  |  HS tracks Right

Victor on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep Right (SSR). This is the whole-character recommendation from TekkenDocs' community sidestep chart (by @Mezerdie), and the lab-tested per-move data below broadly backs it: stepping right beats df4 and df3, two of his most-thrown pokes, plus everything linear.

Three places the default does not hold — know these rather than stepping on autopilot: ub2 tracks SS/W Right (step left instead), IAI.1 tracks right and whiffs left (inside IAI switch to SWL to fish for the whiff), and his Heat Smash tracks right, so during Heat your neutral habit is exactly what it catches.

Stance Counterplay

IAI — Iai Stance

Entered with 3+4, or off f4,2 / uf1,1 / df4,2 / 2,2,2 / qcf2 / fffF+2 / fffF+1+2 / PRF.2,2 / H.db1+2. He CAN block from IAI after 11F. He can cancel to crouch with db. IAI.f parries lows — don't throw lows at a standing Iai stance.

Mid, Mid, SM | i23~24 | +4 | Projectile | Stays in IAI | Powered up in Heat
LOW, LOW | i20~21 | -29 | Unparryable
How to deal with it: His only lows from IAI are IAI.4 (i18, -12) and IAI.d1+2 (i20, -29) — both slow, both punishable. Everything else is mid or high. Default: stand block. When he enters IAI at disadvantage (f4,2 = -12, uf1,1 = -9, IAI.1,1 cancel = -9) his i13 IAI.1 loses to your jab — take your turn. When he enters at advantage (fffF+2 = +5, PRF.2,2 = +5 on hit) respect the mid, look to duck IAI.1+2 on read, and remember IAI.1 whiffs to his left (your SWL).

PRF — Perfumer

Entered with f+3, or off 1,1,2 / 2,1 / b1 / db1,1 / uf1,1 / ub2 / ws1+2 / FC.1+2 with F. He cannot block in PRF. The stance is a punch parry only (frames 5~10, +15~+20 for him on a successful parry). He can cancel to crouch with df (blocks 2F faster) or go to IAI with 3+4 (10F intangible).

How to deal with it: He can't block, and the parry only catches punches. So when he transitions to PRF at disadvantage (b1~F = -5, 2,1~F = -4, db1,1~F = -5, 1,1,2~F = -8, ws1+2~F = -8, uf1,1~F = -10) hit him with a kick, knee, or elbow — anything that isn't a punch is free. Don't jab into PRF; that's exactly what the parry wants. If you'd rather not guess, a fast mid kick also beats PRF.1 (his only low) and PRF.b2 (his only high) cleanly.
