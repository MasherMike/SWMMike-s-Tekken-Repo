Armor King on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk RIGHT (SSR / SWR). Wavu's per-move whiff notes tally six strings that whiff to your right against two that whiff to your left, and the right-side list includes strings he throws constantly: 4,3, the Heat Engager ws3,1, and both Taurus Cutter follow-ups off d+3+4. The @Mezerdie chart also calls SSR, so the two tiers agree. Several of the SSR rows are marked "inconsistent" on Wavu — it's a lean, not a free pass.

Exceptions — these are the ones that catch the SSR habit:
• His 3 high kick (i15) and the BAD.3 power crush both have "good right sidestep tracking" — they follow your SSR. 3 is a high, so duck it instead.
• f+3,2,1 whiffs only to your left, from the 2nd block. db+2,3 also leans left (inconsistent).
• ub+1 Neck Hunter, f,F+3, b+3+4 and db+3 are homing — stepping does nothing; see the list below.

Entered with F off a string or a parry. On block: df+2,1 → +0, 3+4,1 → -8, b+1,2 → -12, 1,2,1 cancel → -19, H.ub+1 → +5. On hit only: f+1+2 (+26g), f,f,n,2 (+8). Raw entry is the f+3+4 punch parry.
Can he block in BAD? TekkenDocs' f+3+4 row says "Cannot block in stance." Wavu doesn't state it for BAD in general — treat it as likely but unconfirmed.

General Counter Play

His parries only catch punches. b+1+3 reverses high and mid punches (parry frames 5~12); f+3+4 parries mid and high punches and goes into BAD — and on a successful parry your throw breaks are disabled for the stun. If he's fishing for either, poke with kicks, knees and lows.
