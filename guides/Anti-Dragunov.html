Anti-Dragunov  |  SSR  |  Tackles Whiff Left

Dragunov on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk RIGHT (SSR / SWR). Every source agrees, and Wavu's own counterplay page spells the direction out in prose three separate times: sidewalk right against his f,f,F+2 (WR2) and back-throw him, SSR against b+1+2, and SSR against SNK.4 because "his Sneak moves tend to be linear." The @Mezerdie sidestep chart also calls SSR, and five of his strike strings are documented whiffing to your right. This is an abundance default — stepping right is actively rewarded.

The exception is his whole tackle family, and it's the one that matters in Heat. Every Ambush Tackle ender — b+4,2,1+2, SS.2,1+2,1+2, and the Heat-only f,f,F+2,H.1+2 / FC.df+1,H.1+2 — whiffs to your left. Out of Heat they're breakable throws (1+2), so your SSR habit costs you nothing: break them. In Heat they become unbreakable, and that's when stepping left (or SWR, for the two H.1+2 versions) is the only way out once the string is running.

Stance Counterplay

SNK — Sneak

Entered raw with qcf, or with DF off 2,1 (-1), 3,1 (-3), ws1 (-5), b+4,2 (-6), f+3 (+0), and FC.df+1,4 (+7 on hit). Stance moves can be delayed 20F; it lapses to standing and can cancel to sidestep with U. Can he block in SNK? Wavu doesn't say — unconfirmed.

How to deal with it: Wavu's own read is the answer — when you see him slip into Sneak off 3,1 or b+4,2, start stepping right, because his Sneak moves are linear. His only low (SNK.3) is -16 and his fastest move is i12, so from the -5/-6 entries your jab wins outright; from the -1 and -3 entries it's close, and SNK.3+4 power crush beats a slow button. Stepping covers all of it.

PGR — Pigeon Roll

Entered with 3+4 (or d+3+4, FC.3+4), or off 2,1 (-3) and df+1 (-2) with 3+4. Crouches frames 6~38 and drops into full crouch from frame 28 — highs whiff against it. Can he block? Not stated.

Heat

Heat Smash | Mid ×3 | i15~16 | +6 oB | 7 chip | Becomes a 50-dmg throw on 1st hit
Plus on block — no punish, don't press. The extension H.2+3,db+1+2 goes into an Ambush Tackle that's unbreakable on hit; interrupt with i15 after blocking the 1st hit
What Heat changes: his throw-enders — Feint & Catch and every Ambush Tackle — become unbreakable and burn Heat time instead. Outside Heat you break them with 1+2. Inside Heat the only answer is to not be standing there: duck the Feint & Catch, step the tackles left (see Movement).

General Counter Play

Range is the matchup. Wavu's summary: he is most devastating point-blank, where he cycles plus-on-block mids and plus-on-hit lows. Stay at least one step out and most of his dangerous tools either whiff or become readable.
