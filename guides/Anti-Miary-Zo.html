Miary Zo on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk LEFT (SSL / SWL) — the opposite of the rest of this database, so don't carry the habit over from a Jin or Bob set.

The reasoning is different too. Against Jin or Bob you step a direction because lots of things whiff there. Against Miary Zo you step left because nothing punishes it: Wavu flags three of her moves as having good right sidestep tracking — MOR.1 (her fastest stance option), 1+2 (a Heat Engager) and b+4 — and flags nothing at all as tracking left. TekkenDocs' community sidestep chart (by @Mezerdie) independently calls SSL for her. The case is absence of risk, not abundance of reward.

Know the exception: her two high jab strings, 1,2,2 and 2,2, whiff to your right. Both are fully high, so ducking beats them as well and keeps you on your default — don't break the SSL habit for these.

Stance Counterplay

MOR — Morengy Miroso

Entered raw with f+3 (r15, actionable after frame 15, stance moves available frames 15~31, can cancel to crouch with DB on frame 16~). Far more often reached automatically off block or hit: 1,1,2~F (-8), 3,1~F (-7), df+1,1~F (±0), f,f,F+2,1~F (+4, on hit OR block), SS.2~F (+4, on hit), b+3,4,2~F (-1, on hit), and WAL.4~F (+5, on hit). No block-restriction is flagged on the wiki for MOR — treat it as a normal blocking stance unlike some characters' offense-only stances.

How to deal with it: His only low is MOR.4 (i17, -12) — slow and clearly launch punishable, so default to standing block. His fastest attack out of the stance is only i13 (MOR.1, -4), so when he enters MOR at real disadvantage (1,1,2~F = -8, 3,1~F = -7, MOR.4 already blocked) your jab wins the race outright. The one entry to actually respect is MOR.1+2 (+6 on block) — since it's slow, look to interrupt it rather than block it, and don't press random buttons into it.

BAO — Baobab Mihira

Entered raw with 3+4 (r20, actionable after frame 20, cancel to r35 with DB on frame 20). Reached automatically off several knee/tornado strings on hit only (df+1,4 / 4,4 / db+2,4 / u+4, all +6), off a blocked Heat Smash (+6, automatic on block only), and off MOR.1+2 (+6, on hit OR block). No block-restriction is flagged for BAO either — treat it as a normal stance.

How to deal with it: He usually arrives in BAO already plus (most transitions land at +6), so don't try to mash a jab into it — respect the first beat. His only low, BAO.4, is slow and disastrous for him if you block it. His fastest option, BAO.1, is High and plus on block — duck it rather than block it. Everything else he has from the stance is Mid, so default to standing block and launch BAO.2 or BAO.4 the moment you see either.

Wall-contextual only. Reached with b+3+4 (r70, transitions on wall contact) or the directional jump variants d+3+4 / u+3+4 (foreground/background leap toward or away from the wall), or for free without a wall during Heat via H.b+3+4.

General Counter Play

Claw flurry chain: f+1+2 (-10) → f+1+2,1+2 (-13) → f+1+2,1+2,3+4 (-22)
Delay windows are minimal (1f on the final input) — punish the earliest hit you catch instead of waiting him out. Letting the chain run just makes the eventual punish bigger while giving him more chances to stop early and stay safe
(HIGH, HIGH) — wr2 alone -9 oB, full string +1 oB and jails
His running elbow — entirely High, duck the whole thing. Blocking it standing hands him a plus frame and a free cancel into MOR at +4 (also listed in Duck and Launch)
Mid | i23~26 | +6~+9 oB(!) | Auto-transitions to BAO
His safe running mid launcher threat — it's a Mid, so you can't duck it pre-emptively. It has to be blocked, and once you do he's plus and picking from Baobab Mihira with initiative (see Stance Counterplay). Don't press random buttons into the block
Mid | i20 | -13 | Punch Sabaki (parry, frames 5~10)
Doubles as her own parry — catches punches only. On a successful parry she gets +30 and a Balcony Break. If she's fishing for this, favor kicks and mids over naked jab pressure
1+2+3+4 Ki Charge — universal Heat-adjacent buff, not a normal attack
While charging she can't block for 5 seconds and takes counter hit on everything during that window — if you ever see it, punish immediately with anything in range
