Hwoarang on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk RIGHT (SSR / SWR) — with a stance-shaped asterisk. Hwoarang is the one character on the @Mezerdie chart with no fixed direction: it marks him SSC*, "usually Hwoarang's moves are stepped away from his chest position" — meaning the answer moves with his feet. Wavu's data is what turns that into a default. Its counterplay page prescribes stepping right three times — against his Right Flamingo follow-ups ("block, then immediately step to the right and block whatever comes next"), against d+3,4, and against the second hit of df+3,4 — and RFS.d+3,3 is documented whiffing to your right. That's Tier 1, and it points right.

The chest rule shows up in his elbows. From his standard stance, b+2 has good right sidestep tracking — it catches SSR. From Right Foot Forward, RFF.b+1 has good left sidestep tracking — it catches SSL. Same move, mirrored by stance. So SSR is cheapest when he's in RFF or Flamingo, and costs you a b+2 when he's standing normally.

The exception that matters most: after RFS.4,3 (+4 on block), his normally-steppable d+3,4 tracks you to either side. Wavu's advice there is to low parry instead of stepping.

Stance Counterplay

The single most important fact about Hwoarang: Wavu notes that in both Flamingo stances he cannot block or crouch. He lives in Flamingo, he usually arrives there plus, and his fastest option out of it is very fast — but if he's idling there, he can be hit.

General Counter Play

Beat the player, not the character. Wavu's overview is blunt: there's "no one set counterplay strategy" against Hwoarang, so the winning approach is reading this opponent's patterns — most Hwoarangs start offense with d+3,4 into Flamingo — and making them second-guess it.
