Eddy on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk RIGHT (SSR / SWR). The @Mezerdie sidestep chart calls SSR for Eddy, and the little per-move data that exists leans the same way: every string Wavu documents as whiffing to a step loses to SWR — RLX.2,4, 1,2,4~3 and 1,2,4~4. Wavu's counterplay page adds the general rule: "Eddy's moves are usually as evasive as they are punishable" — if something isn't punishable on block, it's usually steppable.

The exception is his sweep. db+3 (Rasteira Forte, i28 low) "has good right sidestep tracking" — it's the one move noted to catch your SSR. It's slow and -14c, so the fix is to crouch-block it rather than step it. Also don't step his homing tools — f+3, uf+4 and qcb+4 (list below). Two of the three are highs: duck them instead.

Stance Counterplay

The rule for the whole matchup: Wavu — "It is crucial to not let him loop his stance pressure." HSP and RLX feed into each other (HSP.n,d → RLX, RLX.n,d → HSP). Your job is to find the turn each time he enters one.

HSP — Bananeira (handstand)

Entered raw with f+1+2 / b+1+2, or from strings: 2,3 (-8, per Wavu counterplay), 3,3, b+1,4 (-8), 1,2+F (-5), f,F+4 (-12), f,f,F+3 (+8 on block), H.3+4 (+5), and cancels off f+3,4 (-14), uf+3 (-14), RLX.4,3+4 (-21), b+4,4 (-26), ws4,4,4 (-28).
Can he block? No — Wavu: "Cannot block in stance"; he must drop to RLX to block. Lows: none, except MD.HSP.3+4 once he has Mandinga (-14). Fastest move: HSP.4 at i12 (-1). Watch: HSP.1+2 power crush (pc7~21), HSP.3 crushes highs 6~18, and the HSP.1+3 throw is unbreakable.

RLX — Negativa (relax / low stance)

Entered raw with d+3+4, or from 1,3, b+3,3, ws1,3, d+2,3 (-4c), f+4,3+4, SS.3 (+0), FC.df+3 (+6), ws3+4 (+5c), HSP.1 (+6), HSP.n,d, RLX.1,3 / RLX.2,4+D loops, and the Heat Smash on block (+6).
Can he block? Yes — from frame 20 with B or DB. Lows: RLX.1 (i14, -11c), RLX.3 (i16~17, -26c), RLX.3,3 (-13). Fastest move: RLX.1 at i14. Key property: RLX.1, RLX.3 and RLX.1+2 crush highs from frame 1 — your jab whiffs.

Mandinga — his level-up

Mandinga level rises on hit of db+4,4, uf+1,3, qcb+4, HSP.2,3 and RLX.3,3. At level 1+ his f+1+2 / b+1+2 handstand entries auto-parry lows and go to Ogum Mandinga, and the MD.HSP.3+4 / MD.HSP.b+4 moves unlock (the HSP low, and a +5 mid). Once he's leveled, stop throwing lows at his handstand.
