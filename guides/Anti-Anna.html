Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep RIGHT (SSR). Every "Whiffs vs …" note on Wavu for Anna points the same way — four strings whiff to your right and none to your left. Three of them are her everyday high-starter strings: 1,2,2, 2,2 and 4,2, plus the uf+1,3,3+4 knee ender. The @Mezerdie sidestep chart also calls SSR, so the tiers agree. It is a thin tally (4–0), so treat it as a lean, not a guarantee.

Exceptions — these catch the SSR habit:
• df+4 Wine Opener is "pseudo homing to SSR side" — her i16 low follows your step right. Block it low instead (-15, punishable).
• 1+2, b+2, b+4, f,F+2, HAM.3 and both generic throws are homing — stepping does nothing; see the list below.
• All four SSR rows whiff only from the 1st block — you step after blocking the opener, not on reaction to the ender.

Stance Counterplay

CJM — Chaos Judgement (her low and throw parry)

What it is: b+3 (also HAM.b+3, TOM.3). Parry active frames 3~47; "parries all lows (including Heat Smash lows)" and a throw parry that reverses for 25 damage. Actionable after 16f.
She also recovers into CJM after 3 (-6), 2,3 (-3), ws3 (-3), f,F+3+4 (+0), and can cancel into it with B from df+1,2,3 (-17), df+3,1 (-15), qcf+2,1 (-13), d+4,1 (-10), HAM.3 (-7) and f,F+4 (+6).
So: check CJM with a mid. Lows and throws into it get parried. Wavu doesn't say whether she can block in CJM.
Her only low from it: CJM.d+3, i15, -11.
Fastest options: CJM.2 (i10, high) and CJM.1 (i11~12, high), then CJM.3 (i12~14, mid). Two of the three are highs.
Power crush: CJM.4 (pc7~19, -13). A mid jab-check can get armored; a crouch or a slower mid that recovers in time is the safer check.

General Counter Play

Two parries, two answers. b+3 CJM parries lows and throws (frames 3~47) → check it with mids. b+1+3 Attack Reversal parries high or mid punches or kicks (frames 5~12) → if she's fishing for it, use a low or a throw.
