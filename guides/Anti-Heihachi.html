Heihachi on P1 side — inputs face right

Movement — Which Way to Step

Default: Sidestep / sidewalk LEFT (SSL / SWL) — but treat this as the lowest-confidence default in the database. The @Mezerdie sidestep chart calls SSL. Wavu's per-move data doesn't break the tie: it flags exactly two of his moves as tracking a step, and they point opposite ways. f+3 is "pseudo-homing" to your right; b+4 has good tracking to your left. His counterplay page on Wavu has no prose to settle it. So the chart decides, and the data only tells you what each choice costs.

What SSL costs you: b+4 (Indra's Kick) — i18~19, +30a on hit, +53a on counter hit, and it recovers 11 frames faster on hit or block. That's the bigger of the two tracking threats, which is exactly why this default is soft. What SSR costs you is f+3, a knee that's only -5 on block and +9 on hit. If a Heihachi keeps catching your left step with b+4, switching right is a reasonable read rather than a mistake.

Don't step his stances at all. Both have homing options: RAI.2 out of Thunder God's Kamae and FUJ.1+2 / the unbreakable FUJ.1+3 throw out of Wind God's Kamae. Read the stance instead (see Stance Counterplay).

FUJ — Wind God's Kamae

Entered raw with d+1+2 (actionable after 30F), or with D off 1,2,2 (-9), 3,2 (-2), f+2,2 (-2), f+3 (-2), ws4,4 (-9), f+4 and 1,b+2. FUJ.DF goes into Wind God Step with a low parry on roughly frames 1~14. Can he block in FUJ? Wavu doesn't say — unconfirmed.

How to deal with it: his only low is FUJ.4 (-14), and his fastest move, FUJ.1, is a high — so stand block is the default and ducking covers his quickest jab. He usually enters at -2 to -9, so you often have the turn — but check with a fast mid, not a jab: FUJ.2 crouches under jabs, FUJ.1+2 power-crushes slow buttons, and FUJ.3 jumps your lows. The FUJ.1+3 throw is unbreakable and homing, which is why you can't just sit and block forever. And don't throw lows at him in FUJ — FUJ.DF parries them.

How to deal with it: this is a real mixup, not a free turn. Ducking beats RAI.2 (the +10 high) and probably the throw; standing beats RAI.4. His two mids, RAI.1 and RAI.3, beat a duck — but RAI.3 is only -7, and RAI.1 is -11 if you block it. The default that loses least is stand block and react to the low; duck only when you've seen this player lean on RAI.2.

Heat

Heat Smash | Mid, Mid | i15 | +10 oB | 50 dmg | Reversal Break | 15 chip
Not punishable. On block he goes straight into RAI at +10 (or cancels with B to stay standing at +6). Treat a blocked Heat Smash as the start of the RAI mixup above
What Heat changes: he can throw EWGF and Electric TGF without the just-frame input; Wind God's Kamae gets an attack-reversal against highs and mids (H.d+1+2,P); and he unlocks Warrior Instinct.
