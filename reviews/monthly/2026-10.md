**Grade: B+**

A strong month for the main. Victor went 86–61 (59%) and climbed **+116** (1809 → 1925), a full swing from September's −67, and he won 86 games where the ratings predicted 81.6 (**+4.4**). The climb is real, but you haven't yet proven you can hold 1950+.

### Findings (most important first)

1. **Rating trend: up, and the main moved most.** Victor +116 (start 1809, latest 1925, peak 1990), vs −67 in September. Paul −11 (1710 → 1699) and King −5 (1736 → 1731) were roughly flat; in September King was the one climbing (+132). Victor's last October game was Oct 24, and the Oct 31 session was all Paul. Nov 1 opened 5–5 on Victor at ~1920, so the post-peak plateau is still where you are.

2. **The rank wall holds up as expected losses, not underperformance.** Against opponents 100+ above, Victor went 5–16 vs 4.7 expected (+0.3), and all characters went 9–23 vs 7.7 (+1.3). That's the same picture as the mid-October check (7–12, +2.3), now on a bigger sample. All 17 God of Destruction V games were Victor, at 2–15 against opponents averaging **237 points above** you, with 3.7 wins expected. That is −1.7, slightly under the prediction but not meaningful at this size. Even-rated games (73 of Victor's 147) are where the next 100 points come from, and there you were exactly break-even. One wrinkle: after the peak, Victor went 0–7 vs stronger opponents (1.5 expected) and 19–19 vs even ones.

3. **Problem matchups (Victor, 4+ games): Anna, Hwoarang, Leo.** Anna went 3–5* (4.8 expected), Hwoarang 3–5* (4.1 expected), and Leo 2–5* (2.5 expected, so Leo is mostly tough draws). Combined that's **8–15 vs 11.4 expected (−3.4)**, about the size of the whole post-peak giveback. Each sample is small, so read these as hints. Hwoarang is odd: the Oct 3 guide exists and Paul and King went 6–0 vs him, but Victor is 3–5. The all-character Jack-8 0–4* and Jin 1–4* in the Opponents table are mostly split across characters (Victor 0–2 and 1–2), so I wouldn't act on those yet.

4. **Game 1 vs a new opponent: moved up.** Victor's game 1s went 39–27 (59%), +1.6 vs prediction, against September's 49–51, −3.2. Game 2+ was 47–34 (58%), +2.8. The data can't see which moves you opened with, so I can't credit the safe-poke opener directly. What I can say is that game 1 stopped being a leak in the same month you adopted the plan. Keep it.

5. **Armor King: no longer a problem matchup, but the guide's effect isn't proven.** Victor went 9–8 (53%) vs Armor King in October, 7.5 expected. Jan–Oct is now **20–21**, not the 16–20 in my notes. That figure was computed mid-month. Before the Oct 16 guide Victor was 5–7, after it 4–1*. However, all five after-guide games were against AKs rated 90–230 *below* you (3.5 wins expected), while the pre-guide losses came against GoD III/V AKs rated well above. So the after-guide record is +0.5 above prediction. Nov 1 added a win (≈5–1 since the guide). It's moving the right way, but on 6 games against easier draws.

Also tracked:

- **Final rounds:** Victor 20–22 (48%), down from 51% in September. Jan–Oct is 154–156 (50%), so this month's dip is noise. (Updated count: the "136–138" in my notes predates late October.)
- **Session fatigue:** none this month. Victor was 60% in games 1–10 of a session and 56% from game 11 on. In September the split was 53% / 47%.
- **Week by week:** the week of Oct 12 was the high (36–17, 68%). The Oct 22 session (Victor 5–7, −48 rating) started the slide from the peak.
- **Volume:** 192 ranked games vs 365 in September. Fewer, better games is a good trade.

### Biggest lever for next month (my opinion)

**Clean up the three leaky Victor matchups, starting with Anna.** Ratings say you beat weaker players and lose to stronger ones about as expected. What separates 1925 from 1990+ is the even-rated games, and these matchups are where you're giving away even games.

- **Pros:** It's concrete and measurable, and guides already exist for Anna (Oct 23) and Hwoarang. Anna is also a clean test. The guide arrived after your last Anna game, so every Anna game in November is an honest "after."
- **Cons:** All three samples are under 10 games. A couple of strong repeat opponents could be skewing them, and a month of matchup focus could turn out to be chasing noise. The alternative lever is a session stop-loss to protect peaks (the Oct 22 drop), but the data shows no late-session fade, so I rank it second.
